Little Keys

Sit the baby on your lap in front of the keyboard. Each key he presses makes a bright shape float up, and a moon friend reacts. Low keys make big slow shapes, high keys make small quick ones. With no keyboard nearby, he can tap the iPad screen instead.

  • Keep the keyboard volume low; little ears are sensitive.
  • A few minutes is plenty. The session ends on its own with a calm goodnight screen.
  • To leave, hold the round button in the top corner for a second, so a swat can't close it.

How the app hears you

Touch only. Tap the on-screen keys, or connect the keyboard above.
Which one should I use?
  • iPad or iPhone: use the microphone. Safari can't read USB MIDI cables. Put the device near the keyboard's speakers, keep the room quiet, and play one note at a time.
  • Laptop, Chromebook or Android tablet in Chrome or Edge: connect the keyboard's USB port to the device (an adapter may be needed for USB-C). Detection is exact and chords work.
  • Turn on App sound below only if you're using the cable with the keyboard volume down.
